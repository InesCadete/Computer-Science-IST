# 📊 Analysis and Modelation of Systems (AMS)  

## 📌 Course Overview  
This course focuses on **Systems Engineering** methodologies, **Conceptual Modeling**, and **Requirements Engineering**, covering frameworks like **ISO 42010**, **Zachman**, **MDA**, and **MDE**. Topics include:  

- 🏗 **Systems Engineering**: Architecture, stakeholders, concerns, viewpoints, and models.  
- 🔍 **Requirements Engineering**: Elicitation, validation, and traceability of requirements.  
- 🖥 **Object-Oriented Analysis & Modeling**: UML, SysML, behavior modeling, and structural models.  
- 📜 **Business Process Modeling**: BPMN, process transformations, risk assessment, and enterprise architecture.

## 🏆 Course Project – **BIOGateBox & BioBox**  

### 🚀 **Project Objective**  
Design an **Access Control System** (**BioBox**) for **BIOGateBox (BGB)**, a company selling smart gateways for physical access control. The project is divided into **two stages**, with system modeling using **UML, SysML, BPMN, and ArchiMate**.  

### 📌 **Stage 1: "Viewpoint As-Is" & "Opportunity"**  
✅ **ArchiMate & BPMN Modeling**:  
- **System Context** 🌍 (ArchiMate)  
- **Product Overview** 📦 (ArchiMate)  
- **Order Execution Process (PEP)** 🔄 (BPMN)  

✅ **Opportunity Analysis**:  
- **Business Context Expansion** (ArchiMate)  
- **New Functional Opportunities** (Text + Diagrams)  

### 📌 **Stage 2: Refinement & Expansion**  
✅ **Enhanced UML & SysML Models**  
- **Use Case Diagrams** 🎭 (BGB Gate, BSoft App)  
- **State Machines** ⏳ (Gate Control System)  
- **Sequence Diagrams** 📜 (BGB Gate Operations)  
- **Block Diagrams** 🏗 (SysML representations of BGB Gate)  

✅ **Updated "Viewpoint Opportunity"**  
- **Revised Business Context** 🌐  
- **Use Case Models for BWallet** 💳

## 🏛 **Project Governance & Management**  
BIOGateBox (BGB) follows a **hierarchical structure** with:
