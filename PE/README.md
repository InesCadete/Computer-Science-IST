## 📌 **Course Overview**

The **Probability and Statistics** course covers fundamental concepts and methods in probability theory and statistics, focusing on their application in data analysis and decision-making. The topics include:

1. **Basic Concepts**:
   - Random experiments, sample spaces, events, and interpretations of probability.
   - Axioms of probability, addition rules, conditional probability, multiplication and total probability rules, and independence.
   - Bayes' Theorem and its applications.

2. **Random Variables**:
   - Cumulative distribution functions, discrete and continuous random variables.
   - Mean and variance of random variables.
   - Discrete distributions such as Uniform, Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Hyper-geometric, and Poisson.
   - Continuous distributions like Uniform, Normal, and Exponential.

3. **Joint Probability Distributions and Complements**:
   - Joint, marginal, and conditional probability distributions.
   - Independence, covariance, and correlation.
   - Linear combinations of random variables.
   - The Central Limit Theorem and the Law of Large Numbers.

4. **Sampling and Point Estimation of Parameters**:
   - Data description and random sampling.
   - Point estimation, maximum likelihood estimation, and properties of estimators.
   - Sampling distributions of means and variances.

5. **Interval Estimation of Parameters**:
   - Confidence intervals for parameters in normal distributions and large-sample confidence intervals for other distributions.

6. **Tests of Hypotheses**:
   - Hypothesis testing for parameters in normal distributions, population proportions, goodness of fit, and contingency tables.

7. **Simple Linear Regression and Correlation**:
   - Empirical models, least square estimators, and inference in linear regression models.
   - Coefficient of determination and residual analysis.

The course emphasizes practical data analysis using statistical methods and R programming to apply these concepts in solving real-world problems.

## 📝 **Project Overview**

The project for this course was based on solving a set of **10 statistical and probability problems** using **R programming**. Each question explored different aspects of the course material, ranging from random variables to hypothesis testing. For each question, an attached **PDF document** explained the resolution process in R, providing step-by-step guidance on how the statistical concepts were applied to the problems.

### **Project Outline**:
- **Total Questions**: 10 questions covering a variety of topics such as probability distributions, regression analysis, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals.
- **Software Used**: The project was implemented using **R programming**.
- **Attached PDF**: Each question has an accompanying PDF file that provides detailed explanations and solutions in R.

The questions were designed to help students understand how to apply statistical techniques to real-world data and to learn how to interpret the results produced by statistical models.
